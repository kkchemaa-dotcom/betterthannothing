Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

BetterThanNothing is a workout log that keeps everything on your iPhone. It does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the app stores

The sets you log, their times and notes, and your settings for each exercise. These stay in the app's own storage on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network requests. Nothing you log leaves your device through the app.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that does.
  • It does not read Health data, your location or your contacts.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that backup, as with any app. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

Apple

Apple runs the App Store and keeps its own records of downloads. If you chose in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy. The developer cannot see who you are from it.

Deleting your data

Delete a set in the app by swiping it in History. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: kkchemaa@gmail.com.

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

有做就好是一個把所有紀錄都留在你 iPhone 上的運動日誌。它不收集、不傳送、也不分享任何個人資料。

App 儲存的內容

你記錄的每一組、時間與備註，以及每個動作的設定。這些資料只存在 App 自己在你裝置上的儲存空間。

App 不會做的事

  • 沒有帳號，也不需要登入。
  • 不發出任何網路連線。你記錄的內容不會經由 App 離開你的裝置。
  • 沒有分析、廣告或追蹤，也沒有會做這些事的第三方程式碼。
  • 不讀取健康資料、位置或聯絡人。

備份

如果你用 iCloud 或電腦備份 iPhone，App 的資料會和其他 App 一樣包含在備份中。這些備份由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理。

Apple

App Store 由 Apple 經營，下載紀錄由 Apple 保存。如果你在 iPhone 設定中選擇與 App 開發者分享分析資料，Apple 可能提供匿名的當機報告與使用統計。這些資料由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理，開發者無法從中得知你是誰。

刪除資料

在「紀錄」頁滑動某一組即可刪除。刪除 App 會從裝置上移除它的所有資料。

兒童

App 不向任何人收集資料，包括兒童。

變更

本政策如有變更，新版本會連同新的日期公布在此頁。
